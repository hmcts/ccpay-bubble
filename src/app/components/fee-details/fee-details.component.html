If the document has more than 10 pages, you must also choose a fee for the additional pages.

For example: Your client wants 3 copies of a 20 page document.

You select the fee for the first 10 pages. It costs £10 for the first 10 pages (£10 x 3 = 30).

You’d need to enter 3 copies.

You select the fee for the additional pages. It costs 50p for each subsequent page.

Step 1: £10 x 3 copies = £30.

Step 2: Select the fee for the additional pages (if more than 10 pages). For example, 10 additional pages at 50p = £5.

Step 3: £5 x 3 copies = £15. You’ll need to enter this against the new fee.
